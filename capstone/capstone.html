my time as an eta:
the work

click to open

i know now that i’m exactly where i want to be... and that my time as an ETA is just the beginning.

when i had called my mom to tell her that i’d gotten this position, i managed to articulate through tears of joy that it would be incorrect to say that all of my hard work had finally paid off because it felt like the beginning of everything i’ve ever wanted, not an ending.

after being able to walk the halls of the the new york office and contribute towards the incredible work done by the folks at warner, i’ve lived by those words each and every day! no matter how expansive or minute my given task in a day has been, it’s felt so rewarding.

it’s been the honor of a lifetime to learn from the folks that make the music world turn, and to help them along the way has been truly wonderful.

my time as an eta:
the takeaway

let your passion propel you forward.

if you’re anything like me, you were drawn to warner because you listen to music in the same way you breathe: it’s constant and necessary to your being.

hold fast to that love, and it will charge every assignment and task you complete.

my missive to a future eta

i hope i’m still inhaling music and exhaling illustrations, photographs, designs, and fervent texts to my friends the way i am now.

i hope the incessant need to create when i hear a song that strikes a chord within me, an explosive and bright instinct that doesn't leave until it's fully expressed, never dulls.

i hope i’m decades into my time in new york city, decades into a career in the music that completes my world, and looking back on decades’ worth of work to be endlessly proud of. and, if i may indulge myself in a bit of luxury, i hope i'm living in an apartment with in-unit laundry. a girl’s gotta dream.

i hope, before all else, that future sophia has taken where i am presently as a charge to her motivation and pursued her dreams relentlessly... and the work towards that begins today. :)
